# C360 — Visa (public reconstruction)

Visa's "customer" in public diagrams is the **issuer / acquirer / fintech**, plus a cardholder credential. Do not draw a consumer CRM.

## Identity
- Credential / PAN token / account on the foundation layer (2025 ARS: ~12B cards/accounts/wallets).
- Client (FI) identity is a network participant, not a HubSpot contact.

## Channels
- Visa as a Service access layer: APIs, MCP server, managed solutions.

## Marketing
- Visa as a Service / Intelligent Commerce is the public GTM. Internal MAP **unknown**.

## Service
- NOC + ROC are *network* ops, not a customer-service desk.
- Client support stack **unknown**.

## Ops that write the record
- Authorization + BASE II clearing/settlement + STIP.
- That is a *transaction* 360.

## Plausible, unsourced
- Salesforce-class CRM for Visa client coverage.
